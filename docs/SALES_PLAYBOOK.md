# FastLead Sales Playbook

## Target Customer
Solo and small construction trades: roofers, electricians, HVAC, plumbers, painters, general contractors doing $200K-$2M/year revenue.

## Pain Points to Highlight
1. "How many leads have you lost because you were on a job site?"
2. "When someone fills out your form at 11pm, do they get a response?"
3. "How long does it take you to call back a lead?"

## Cold Call Script

```
Hi [NAME], this is [YOUR NAME] from FastLead.

Quick question — when someone fills out a form on your website or emails you for a quote,
how fast do you typically get back to them?

(Pause. Let them answer.)

We built a system that instantly responds to every lead, alerts you within seconds,
and tracks everything in a mobile dashboard. Would you be open to seeing how it works?

(If yes, send dashboard demo link.)
```

## Pricing
- **Beta:** $197/month (lock-in forever price)
- **Value prop:** "One lost $5,000 roofing job pays for 2 years of FastLead"
- **Trial:** 7-day free trial, no credit card

## Objection Handling

**"I already have a website form"**
> Does it instantly reply to the customer and alert you within seconds? Most forms just send an email that gets buried.

**"I can't afford $197/month"**
> How much is one average job worth to you? $3,000? $5,000? If we help you close just one extra lead this year, you've paid for the whole service.

**"I don't have a website"**
> We actually have a package for that. We can build you a simple lead-capture site that feeds directly into FastLead.

## Demo Strategy
1. Create a demo client in the system
2. Send yourself a test lead through the webhook
3. Show the instant email alert on your phone
4. Show the live dashboard updating
5. Let them tap-to-call the lead
